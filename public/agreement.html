01 Scope of Work & Deliverables

The Developer agrees to design, develop and implement the following functionality for the Client, subject to the requirements and approvals agreed by both parties.

  • Centralized Office Backend: Development of a centralized backend for linked management of the first and second custom PHP websites.
  • Multi-Tier Pricing Engine: Implementation of three pricing levels: Office Cost, Site 1 Price, and Site 2 Markup / Discount Price.
  • Inventory & Stock Synchronization: Synchronization of stock status between the connected websites, including in-stock and out-of-stock indicators and the agreed automatic display or hiding of prices and related buttons.
  • Quotation Page Optimization: Implementation of agreed loading optimizations for quotation pages.
  • Label Printing Integration: Integration with BarTender software for label printing, including the agreed formats with and without a logo, subject to compatible software, licenses, hardware and access being provided.

Any feature, third-party integration, design revision or functionality not expressly described in this section is outside the agreed scope unless approved in writing by both parties.
