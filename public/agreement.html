Key terms

1 Scope. The Developer builds, deploys and hands over the website described above. Extra work is quoted in writing and starts only after approval.
2 Git. Source code is kept in a Git repository, preferably in the Client's own account. Secrets are never committed.
3 Vercel. The site is deployed on Vercel and linked to Git: pushes to main go live, other branches create previews. The Vercel project is owned by, or transferred to, the Client at handover.
4 Payment. Invoices are due in 7 days. The deposit is non-refundable once work starts. Domain, Vercel plan and other third-party costs are paid by the Client.
5 Acceptance. The Client reviews the staging preview within 5 business days. No reply means accepted. Delays in content or feedback move the dates.
6 Ownership. On full payment the Client owns the custom code and designs. Open-source and the Developer's existing tools keep their own licences.
7 Warranty. Defects against this scope are fixed free for 30 days after delivery. Ongoing support is a separate plan.
8 General. Both parties keep project information confidential. Liability is capped at fees paid. Either side may end this with 14 days' written notice; work done is paid for.
